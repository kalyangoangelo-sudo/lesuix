About Us

Nurturing Hearts & Minds
Since 2017

Lisieux Infant School Kabojja is a mixed Nursery and Pre-Primary school, officially opened by its patron Rev. Fr. Kibalama Denis Warren in 2017. We run from Baby Class to Primary Three, offering quality Early Childhood Development (ECD) education guided by faith, creativity, and care.

Our Classes

Every Stage,
Every Child

From the tiniest toddler to our confident Primary Three pupils — we have a warm, structured learning environment for every age.

👶

Baby Class

Ages 2 – 3

A warm and playful introduction to school life with a focus on socialisation and sensory play.

  • Sensory play & exploration
  • Songs, rhymes & stories
  • Social skills development
🌱

Middle Class

Ages 3 – 4

Structured play-based learning that introduces foundational literacy and numeracy concepts.

  • Letter & number recognition
  • Creative arts & crafts
  • Group activities & games
⭐

Top Class

Ages 4 – 5

Building confidence and independence as children prepare for primary school transition.

  • Reading & writing readiness
  • Basic mathematics
  • Science exploration
📚

Primary One – Three

Ages 6 – 9

A solid early primary education grounded in the national curriculum with a nurturing approach.

  • English, Maths & Science
  • Social Studies & RE
  • Physical Education
